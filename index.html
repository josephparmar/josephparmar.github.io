About Me

My name is Joseph Parmar, I am an MPhys Physics with Astrophysics student at the University of York. I have interests in many areas of physics, however particularly in particle and detector physics.


After my A-Levels, I took a gap year and worked at Reigate College as a Physics Laboratory Technician, where I gained key skills in lab technique, as well as working and communicating in a team.


In the summer between my first and second years of University, I was accepted into an internship at the University of York, where I worked on optimising boost converters to be used to bias silicon photomultipliers, and implementing temperature controlled bias voltages to match the SiPM breakdown voltage’s temperature variance.


I have also been on the University of York Mixed 1s Fencing in both my first and second years, and was captain of the team in the 25/26 year, during which our team won the historic annual Roses varsity against Lancaster University.


I will be graduating in 2028, after which I am planning on pursuing a PhD in Physics. This website contains my formal academic experience, as well other personal projects I have taken on out of interest. I may also publish any dissertations and reports here as my research continues.
